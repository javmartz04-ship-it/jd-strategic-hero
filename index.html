About Josh
Second Generation · Raised in a franchise family

Raised in franchising. Now on your side of the table.

Josh DuBois didn't learn franchising from a book. His family started as independent restaurant owners, became multi-unit franchisees, then crossed to the franchisor side and helped scale a dry-cleaning brand from around ten locations to more than 2,400 worldwide.

Josh grew up inside that story. He was managing one of the family's franchises while he was still in high school, then spent years in corporate finance and recruiting, learning how deals actually get evaluated and how people actually get hired.

Today he's FranServe-certified, opening his own franchise, and sitting on your side of the table full time. He's lived the franchisee seat, the franchisor seat, and the operator's seat, so when he vets a brand for you, he knows exactly what he's looking at.

"I've lived every side of it, the good deals and the bad ones."
